Real Estate Guidance.
Built on Fundamentals.

Real estate decisions carry an emotional and financial weight that rarely translates onto a standard balance sheet. Long before stepping into professional advisory, I experienced property decisions from the other side of the table—as a consumer, homeowner, and investor. I understand the quiet pressure of evaluating numbers that directly impact everyday living, long-term security, and family plans. That perspective permanently informs how I practice.

Over the past half-decade, the Toronto market has moved through extreme cycles: rapid rate swings, evolving regulatory frameworks, and constant media volatility. In periods of high market friction, speculative hype and aggressive sales tactics become liabilities. What remains durable are disciplined fundamentals—evaluating intrinsic asset quality, interrogating micro-market data, and possessing the patience to walk away when the numbers fail to protect downside risk.

Advisory is not a transactional function; it is a long-term position. My role is to serve as an objective sounding board—delivering clear-eyed counsel whether the prudent move is to execute, negotiate, or pause. Supported by the analytical bench of The Path Real Estate Team and the collective infrastructure of RE/MAX Hallmark, my work is built around one standard: protecting your capital and decisions with uncompromised clarity.
